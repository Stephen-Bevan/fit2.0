Why FIT AI

Build AI capability within your workforce

Our pathway pairs you with AI training from the world's leading technology companies - matched to every role and skill level, from introductory to advanced.

  • Ready for an AI-enabled future

    Equip your people with the skills an AI-driven workplace increasingly demands.

  • Every role and skill level

    From non-technical staff getting started to developers going advanced.

  • Backed by industry leaders

    Training delivered with Microsoft, Google, IBM, Intel and Accenture.

  • Tailored to your needs

    Built around your organisation's roles, ambitions and skill gaps.
