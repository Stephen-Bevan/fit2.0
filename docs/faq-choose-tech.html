Who can take part in Choose Tech?

Choose Tech is for 15–18 year olds in DEIS schools and 15–20 year olds in Youthreach centres, and it runs with schools, centres and groups nationwide.

Does it cost anything?

No. Choose Tech is free for participating schools, Youthreach centres and training centres.

What does the programme involve?

Four parts: FIT Career Pathways Workshops, 70+ bite-sized online modules students work through at their own pace, information packs for educators and students, and Near Peer Tech Engagements with tech professionals — virtual or in the classroom.

Full detail sits on the Choose Tech programme page.

How do we register our group?

Educators and Group Leaders register first via the FIT unique link, then set up the organisation and a class group with its own URL or class code. Students join with that URL, or by adding your Organiser Code to their profile.

The five steps are set out on the Choose Tech page, and there is a short how-to video.

Do students need parental consent?

The digital age of consent in Ireland is 16 years. It is the responsibility of the school or organisation to seek permission from a parent or guardian for student participation.

What do students get out of it?

Students earn digital badges and awards that build their tech knowledge and digital literacy, and they can work towards Bronze, Silver and Gold recognition. A good starting point is the FIT Digital Pathways Badge (Worker section), which covers tech roles, opportunities and pathways.

What is the FIT Tech Ambassador Award?

Educators can certify as a FIT Tech Apprenticeship Ambassador and earn Bronze, Silver or Gold recognition for their school or centre. Badges are issued via Credly. The online questionnaire is the first step.

Can Choose Tech count towards a Gaisce President’s Award?

Yes. FIT is a Gaisce Challenge Partner, so students can learn about the world of tech and work towards their Gaisce President’s Award through Choose Tech. Email choosetech@fit.ie to get started.

What support do we get once registered?

Each organisation is assigned a member of the FIT Choose Tech Team, who will help you register and arrange dates for the FIT workshops and Near Peer Engagements. Email choosetech@fit.ie with your name, contact number and email address.

Who supports the programme?

The Choose Tech programme is supported by JPMorganChase.

Programme detail, testimonials and highlights sit on the Choose Tech page.
