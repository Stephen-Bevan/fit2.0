Funders

Who funds FIT's work.

FIT programmes are funded and supported by State bodies and national agencies. SOLAS is FIT's principal funder, with delivery co-ordinated nationally through the Education and Training Boards.

SOLAS

The State body responsible for funding, planning and co-ordinating Further Education and Training (FET) in Ireland, and for the National Apprenticeship system.

Visit SOLAS →

QQI

Quality and Qualifications Ireland is the independent State agency responsible for promoting quality and accountability across Irish education and training.

Visit QQI →

ETBI

Education and Training Boards Ireland is the national representative body for Ireland's sixteen Education and Training Boards, who deliver FIT programmes locally.

Visit ETBI →

Department of Education and Skills

Supports high-quality education that enables people to reach their full potential and contribute to Ireland's social and economic development.

Visit the Department →

Generation Apprenticeship

The national campaign promoting apprenticeships across Ireland as a valuable route to a professional qualification and career.

Visit Generation Apprenticeship →
