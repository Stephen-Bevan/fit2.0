What we do

Building pathways into tech careers

At FIT NI, we co-design tailored academies with employers who will hire programme participants. This ensures learners develop the exact technical and professional skills required from day one, and bridges the gap between training and employment.

Our goal is simple: to expand and strengthen the tech talent pool for everyone. We open new pathways into tech for people who are underrepresented, changing careers or returning to work.
