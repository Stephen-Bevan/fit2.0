What is a tech apprenticeship?

Your career in tech starts here

FIT's Tech Apprenticeship programmes combine structured training with real workplace experience.

Over two years you build in-demand tech skills and gain an industry-recognised qualification while working with an employer — earning from day one. Choose from Software Development, Computer Networking or Cybersecurity, with more paths to follow.

  • 2 year programme70% workplace learning

Choose your career path

Your first step into
a tech career

Four distinct career paths. Each leads to a QQI Level 6 Advanced Certificate, earned while working with an employer.

  • NFQ Level 6

    Computer Networking

    Build and look after the networks that keep an organisation connected — routers, switches, servers and cloud infrastructure.

    View course →
  • NFQ Level 6

    Software Development

    Write, test and maintain the code behind websites, apps and business systems, working in a real development team.

    View course →
  • NFQ Level 6

    Cybersecurity

    Protect systems and data — spotting threats, responding to incidents and keeping an organisation secure day to day.

    View course →
  • NFQ Level 6 · Coming soon

    Data Analytics

    Turn raw data into clear reports and dashboards that help a business understand what is happening and decide what to do next.

Eligibility requirements

  • ✓

    18 years or over

  • ✓

    Five Leaving Certificate subjects at O6/H7, or an NFQ Level 5 qualification

  • ✓

    Maths and English at Ordinary Level, or equivalent

  • ✓

    Eligible for FET programmes — currently only Stamp 4, 5 or 6

  • ✓

    Entitled to live, study and work in Ireland

  • ✓

    Currently residing in Ireland

Application process

Five steps to your
dream tech career

Applications are open all year round. Once recruitment opens for your chosen programme, we'll be in touch!

Apply now → Get Interview Ready →
  1. 01

    Apply

    Apply for your chosen Apprenticeship career path.

  2. 02

    Submit eligibility documents

    The FIT team determine whether you're eligible for the programme.

  3. 03

    Complete aptitude test

    Attend a scheduled information and aptitude session.

  4. 04

    FIT interview

    Get support with CV and interview preparation.

  5. 05

    Employer interview

    Successful employer interviews then lead to a place on the apprenticeship programme.
