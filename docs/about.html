Who we are

Growing Ireland's tech talent pipeline.

Established in 1999, FIT is dedicated to addressing the skills requirements of Ireland's increasingly digitalised economy.

Promoting inclusion and diversity, FIT consistently demonstrates that wider access to tech skills training leads to exciting career paths and quality employment for anyone with a curiosity and passion for technology.

Our mission is to promote an inclusive and sustainable smart economy — ensuring access to in-demand tech skills for quality employment and shared prosperity.

What we do

Five ways we widen access to tech.

01

Upskilling job seekers

Practical programmes that help people gain in-demand tech skills and return to quality work.

02

Increasing access to training

Delivered through the ETBs nationally, opening in-demand technology training to a diverse cohort.

03

Promoting smart people, smart skills

Championing the talent that inclusive training produces to employers across Ireland.

04

Training for employment

Supporting people to commence careers, from first step through to a role in tech.

05

Putting policy in action

Turning national skills and inclusion policy into programmes that reach real people.

Governance

Board of Directors

FIT is a company limited by guarantee, guided by a Board of Directors drawn from industry, education and the public sector.

Our board provides strategic direction and oversight, ensuring FIT delivers on its mission with integrity, transparency and lasting impact for the communities we serve.

FIT EU

Our European work.

FIT works with European partners to design and test skills programmes that respond to shared priorities — digital inclusion, green skills and pathways into employment.

European projects let FIT trial new approaches with partners across member states, then bring what works back into national delivery with the Education and Training Boards.

  • Co-funded projects with partners across the EU
  • Focus on green skills, digital inclusion and employability
  • Findings feed back into FIT programmes in Ireland
