Why employers choose FIT

Developing the workforce of tomorrow.

For over 25 years, FIT has worked with employers across Ireland to address skills shortages, develop future talent and build stronger workforces.

Through Tech Apprenticeships, workforce development programmes and industry-led training, we help organisations build the skills they need for long-term success.
