About the expo

What is the HERo in Tech Expo?

FIT's annual HERo in Tech is a unique event designed to showcase the vast opportunities available to women in technology.

The event brings together leading companies, industry experts and inspiring female role models to engage directly with female students, sharing insights into diverse career paths.

  • Female students from schools and colleges across Ireland
  • Exhibitor stands, expert talks and interactive demos
  • School and exhibitor registration open for 2026
