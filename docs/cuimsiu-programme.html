Co-funded programme

This project is co-funded by the Government of Ireland through the Department of Social Protection and the European Union.

WorkAbility programme

Flexible support for disabled and neurodivergent people.

Cuimsiú helps people build confidence, explore technology careers, meet employers and access practical supports at a pace that works for them.

16+Open to people aged sixteen and over.
60Programme places available each year.
Mar-NovEvents, mentoring and employer connections.

Build confidence

Support participants to understand their strengths, interests and practical next steps.

Explore tech careers

Introduce training routes, workplace expectations and realistic opportunities in technology.

Connect with employers

Create inclusive employer links through events, mentoring, advice and workplace preparation.

Your journey

A flexible support path.

From March to November, the Cuimsiú team runs events, workshops and training while providing one-to-one support through Employability Mentors.

  • 01Tech training, platforms and practical workshops
  • 02CV, interview and workplace preparation
  • 03Assistive technology and self-advocacy support
  • 04Employer networking and career exploration
