Who it's for

Working with educators to widen access to tech.

FIT partners with secondary schools, colleges, Youthreach centres and training providers across Ireland to bring technology careers within reach of every student.

  • Free, flexible programmes that fit around your timetable
  • Digital badges, certifications and awards for students
  • A named FIT contact supporting your school or centre
