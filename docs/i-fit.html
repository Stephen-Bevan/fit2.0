All-Female Intake
The programme

What is the I‑FIT
apprenticeship?

I-FIT is a two-year programme enabling women to gain the tech skills and qualifications to start a successful ICT career.

Delivered as an all-female Software Development Associate intake, I-FIT combines the same industry-recognised, work-based training as FIT's mainstream apprenticeships with a peer network of women learning and starting their careers together.

All apprenticeships Apply here →
  • Women, all backgroundsNo tech experience needed
  • NFQ Level 6Industry recognised qualification
  • 2 years, paidEarn while you learn from day one
  • Annual intakeApplications open all year round
Why I-FIT

Ireland needs more female tech talent

I-FIT brings together women at the start of their technology careers, creating a community where you can learn, collaborate and grow alongside people on a similar journey.

You'll build your technical skills, share experiences and learn from one another. It helps you gain the confidence and workplace experience to take your next step in tech.

FIT recruits all year round. Apply any time and you'll be invited to an information and aptitude session.

Application process

Five steps to your
place on I-FIT

Applications are open all year round. Once recruitment opens for the next I-FIT intake, we'll be in touch.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Send your application for the I-FIT Software Development Associate Apprenticeship.

  2. 02

    Submit eligibility documents

    The FIT team determine whether you're eligible for the programme.

  3. 03

    Complete aptitude test

    Attend a scheduled information and aptitude session.

  4. 04

    FIT interview

    Get support with CV and interview preparation.

  5. 05

    Employer interview

    A successful employer interview leads to your place on the programme.
