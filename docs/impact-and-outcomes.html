Why it matters

Building skills & creating opportunities.

In 2025, 6,500+ people across Ireland engaged with FIT’s programmes and supports, building the skills, confidence and experience to take their next step.

From aspiring tech apprentices and job-seekers to career changers and mature learners, our programmes create pathways into skills, employment and further education, while connecting employers with the talent they need.

Behind the numbers

Impact in 2025

6,500+ people engaged with FIT nationwide, across two key areas of our work:

The Tech Apprenticeship programme continued to attract strong learner interest and employer demand throughout 2025.

Employability and training supports continued to provide accessible pathways for learners and job-seekers pursuing employment and further education opportunities. This included Core, Cuimsiú and Choose Tech supports.

Programme outcomes
36%of applicants secure tech apprenticeship placements

Participant demographics

Broad and diverse
talent pool

Our 2025 participant data shows a broad and diverse talent pool,
with representation across age groups, educational backgrounds
and every region of Ireland.

43% of participants were aged 34+

A significant proportion of our participants are mature learners, career changers and people looking to reskill.

33% of participants were female

Women currently make up approximately 24% of the ICT workforce in Ireland — our programmes and initiatives are helping to close the gender gap in technology.

54% of participants based outside of Leinster

FIT reaches participants across every region of Ireland, helping us to extend access to technology skills and employment opportunities nationwide.

Impact across schools

Supporting Schools & Colleges

FIT works directly with schools and FET colleges to open up technology careers before subject and course choices are made.

Core and Choose Tech show students the roles available in tech and the routes into them, while Hero in Tech brings students face to face with women working in the industry.
