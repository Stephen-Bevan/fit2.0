About FIT

A practical bridge between potential and employment.

Since 1999, FIT has been helping people access meaningful careers in technology while supporting employers to build future-ready workforces.

FIT (Fastrack into Information Technology) is a representative organisation of the technology sector, committed to growing Ireland's tech talent pipeline in close collaboration with education, training and community partners.

  • ✓ 250,000+ learners trained
  • ✓ 120,000+ progressed into employment
  • ✓ 300+ employers nationwide
