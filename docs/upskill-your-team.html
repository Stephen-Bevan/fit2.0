Why upskill your team

Build the skills
you need next

Technology is changing fast. Give your employees the skills to keep pace with fully funded training from FIT.

FIT helps employers across Ireland build in-demand technology skills within their existing workforce through a two year Tech Apprenticeship programme. With fully funded training for your team in Computer Networking, Cybersecurity, Software Development and Data Analytics.

How does it work?

From skills gap to qualified team

FIT's Tech Apprenticeship allows employees to gain new technical skills while remaining an active part of your workforce.

  1. 01

    Skills needs analysis

    We map the capability you have today against the roles you need to fill.

  2. 02

    Choose the right programme

    Pick the discipline that matches your business needs.

  3. 03

    Your employee continues working with you

    They stay in role and in your team throughout.

  4. 04

    Training completed over two years

    Either in-person or hybrid, depending on the programme and what works best for your organisation.

  5. 05

    Qualified in two years
