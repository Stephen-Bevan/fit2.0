Pre-Tech Apprenticeships

What is a Pre-Tech Apprenticeship?

Pre-Tech Apprenticeships prepare more people for the Tech Apprenticeship route to careers in IT by showcasing tech roles and providing skills sampling in growth areas such as Software Development, Computer Networking and Cybersecurity.

Who is the programme for?

It is aimed at candidates who wish to pursue a Tech Apprenticeship but are unsure of which programme to choose, or who may not currently satisfy the eligibility criteria.

What will I do on the programme?

You engage in practical assignments that focus on both your technical skills and your professional development, and you gain greater insight into what is required to successfully complete a Tech Apprenticeship.

You also get to determine whether the programme is for you and which stream is your preferred option: Software Development, Computer Networking or Cybersecurity.

Does it help me get onto a Tech Apprenticeship?

It lays the foundations of your career in tech, enhances your employability in the tech sector and improves your prospects of securing a position on a Tech Apprenticeship programme.

Candidates who have completed a FIT recognised Pre-Tech Apprenticeship can also use the Recognition of Prior Learning route where they do not hold a suitable Leaving Certificate, by furnishing evidence of the programme along with a copy of their Junior Certificate parchment. A place is not automatic — host employers select the apprentices they take on.

What level is the pre-apprenticeship course?

The pre-apprenticeship IT Technician course runs at NFQ Level 5 and builds the core skills and confidence to step into a Level 6 tech apprenticeship. Current routes and locations are listed on the courses page.

How do I apply?

Apply online through the FIT application form, or contact the team to talk through which route suits you best.

Programme details sit on the Tech Apprenticeships page.
