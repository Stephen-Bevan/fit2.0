FIT programmes

Earn while you learn. FIT tech apprenticeships combine off-the-job skills training with on-the-job experience, leading to a nationally recognised qualification. Seven programmes run under FIT itself: four Level 6 tech apprenticeships, a Level 5 pre-apprenticeship, and manufacturing maintenance in Navan and Ballina.

NFQ Level 6 · Apprenticeship

Computer Networking Associate

Design, build and support the networks that keep organisations connected — a two-year, work-based apprenticeship.

How a tech apprenticeship works

Apply

Register your interest and complete a short application — no degree required.

Match

We help you find a host employer and start earning from day one.

Train

Combine off-the-job classroom learning with on-the-job experience.

Qualify

Achieve a nationally recognised NFQ Level 6 qualification and progress.
