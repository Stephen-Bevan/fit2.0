About the programme

Inspiring young people into tech, earlier.

The ‘Choose Tech’ programme, supported by JPMorganChase, seeks to engage young people (15-20yr olds) into the exciting world of tech at an earlier stage. The programme aims to inspire young people to learn new skills and develop existing ones, and to provide support and information on pathways into a tech career, including pre-tech and tech apprenticeships

This free programme addresses the structural under-representation of socio-economically disadvantaged young people and increases diversity in the pre-tech & tech apprenticeships and the tech sector.

Steps to register

Registering your group takes five simple steps

For groups that meet the criteria: 15–18 year olds in a DEIS school, or 15–20 year olds in Youthreach.

  1. 01

    Educators register first

    Educators and Group Leaders register via the FIT unique link — name, date of birth, email and a password (8+ characters, 1 symbol, 1 number).

  2. 02

    Register your school or organisation

    Click ‘Organisers’, enter your organisation name and type, then ‘New Group’ to set up your class with its own URL or class code.

  3. 03

    Students join via your URL

    Share your organisation's unique URL. Students enter a nickname, date of birth*, email address and their own password.

  4. 04

    Or join via class code

    Students can instead register at www.idea.org.uk/register, then add your ‘Organiser Code’ under Edit Profile.

  5. 05

    Log in & track progress

    Log in at www.idea.org.uk — admin users track group progress in the dashboard tab.

*The digital age of consent in Ireland is 16 years. It is the responsibility of the school/organisation to seek permission from a parent/guardian for student participation.
